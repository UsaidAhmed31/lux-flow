13

Spacing system

8-pt scale with 4-pt steps for tight internals. Components use semantic tokens; breakpoints change tokens, never components.
4812162432404864

Rules: one owner per gap (a parent’s gap or the next sibling’s margin, never both). Less space inside a group than between groups. Pages share one left edge; reading pages cap line length at 680px instead of re-centring.
